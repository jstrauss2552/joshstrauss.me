Dental technology

GumGauge

Co-founder & CEO · Aug 2023–present

Investigational chairside periodontal-measurement concept built around a bite-held mouthpiece and companion software. No hardware or clinical performance data exists; session timing, safety, and performance remain unvalidated design targets. The device is not cleared or approved by the FDA, and its premarket pathway remains under review. Alongside running the company, I build the public website and sample-data software demo.

A little
background.

Austin, Miami, and a few places in between.

Josh Strauss is a mission-driven builder and founder based in Austin, TX & Miami, FL. He is the co-founder & CEO of GumGauge and a sophomore at the University of Austin (UATX), shipping software end to end across native apps and private-capital fintech.

The reason behind all of it is simple: he wants to help people and leave the world better than he found it. That thread runs from nine years of youth human-rights advocacy with B’nai B’rith International, from delegations to the UN in Geneva and UNESCO in Paris to leading a youth mission to Colombia, through growing up working events for the family production studio, to the ventures he builds today as a sophomore at UATX, where he studies economics, politics, and history.

Getting there meant leaving Northeastern after one semester: a deliberate bet on an unaccredited school and the like-minded people in it, the kind who constantly and willingly challenge themselves to push the limits of what it means to be an active member of society.

Right now

Running GumGauge, iterating on Mise’s iOS TestFlight beta, validating Giltedge & Co.’s pre-seed thesis, developing Edgeboard, and using and refining Jarvis on my Mac.
